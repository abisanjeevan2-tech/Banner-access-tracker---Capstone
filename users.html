Import Users from CSV

CSV Format Requirements:
  • First row must be the header: cwid,username,password,email,role
  • All fields are required including email
  • Role must be one of: Grantee, Grantor, Administrator, SuperUser
  • CWID must be 8 digits
  • Example row: 12345678,jdoe,password123,jdoe@winthrop.edu,Grantee

Remove Grantees by CSV

Upload a CSV to permanently remove grantee accounts and all their associated requests. This action cannot be undone.

CSV Format:
  • First row must be the header: cwid or username (or both)
  • Only Grantee role accounts will be removed — other roles are protected
  • All requests submitted by removed grantees will also be deleted
  • Example: cwid,username then 12345678,jdoe
